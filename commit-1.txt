HID: logitech: add an opt-in CU0019 radio debugfs companion

The 046d:c542 C-U0019 receiver works with hid-generic but does not expose
its vendor radio registers through standard HID reports. Add an optional
debugfs companion for studying reception problems without replacing the
normal HID and USB drivers or adding a stable userspace ABI.

Observe the HID bus with a non-exclusive subsys_interface. Restrict
active access to USB bcdDevice 0302, feature report 5 and five fixed
four-byte register words. Validate the reply signature and observed
DMA/RF layout before publishing raw RSSI and a PLL frequency candidate.
Do not infer dBm, mouse presence, packet loss or RF measurement age.

Keep reads disabled by default, on demand, root-only and rate limited.
Share one cache between radio and raw-register views, invalidate failed
samples, and stop probing after a transport/protocol failure. Do not
resume a suspended receiver. Use device references, transport trylocks
and independent per-open snapshots to separate debugfs and device
lifetimes. No background worker or device-driver binding is added.

This RFC requests feedback on the companion architecture and exclusive
feature-channel arbitration. Its mutex does not serialize vendor-feature
requests from unrelated hidraw clients; concurrent use is unsupported.
Physical unplug and system suspend stress testing remain outstanding.
